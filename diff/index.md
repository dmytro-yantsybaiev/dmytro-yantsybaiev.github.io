# Diff: Merge Request Review

Support and legal documents for the iOS app Diff: Merge Request Review, a merge request client that works with GitLab™.

## Support

For questions, bug reports or help with the App, email [d.yantsybaiev@gmail.com](mailto:d.yantsybaiev@gmail.com). It helps to say which iPhone or iPad and iOS version you use, and whether you sign in to gitlab.com or to another GitLab server.

- **Subscriptions.** Manage or cancel your subscription in your device's Settings, under your Apple Account, in Subscriptions. Deleting the App does not cancel it.
- **Refunds.** Refunds are handled by Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). Your statutory rights are unaffected, and you can also contact me.
- **Complaints.** See [section 7 of the Terms of Use](./terms/#7-support-and-complaints).

Po polsku: pytania i reklamacje można wysyłać na adres e-mail [d.yantsybaiev@gmail.com](mailto:d.yantsybaiev@gmail.com) (zob. [§ 7 Warunków korzystania](./pl/terms/#7-wsparcie-i-reklamacje)).
